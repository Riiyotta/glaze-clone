## 2.0.0 — 2026-10-24 — every route of the clone, and PageSpec `mode: reference | generated` (PageSpec-breaking: `pageSpecVersion` 2.0.0)

Two problems with 1.1.0. (1) It modelled 7 of the clone's 294 routes: the clone now has 15 page components (AppPage, Aup, Awards, AwardsTerms, BlogIndex, BlogPost, Changelog, Download, Landing, LegalPage, NotFoundPage, Pricing, Privacy, Store, Terms) behind 19 route declarations, and the README said the rest were out of scope. ### `mode: reference | generated` (PageSpec-breaking)
- **`mode`** is required, a closed enum, on every PageSpec; the schema and `registry.manifest.json` carry `pageSpecVersion` 2.0.0, now machine-checked against each other, and `repositoryVersion` against the newest CHANGELOG heading.
- **Asset roles 10 -> 30, each with `availability`**: 12 `reference-only`, 5 `blocked-for-generation` (logo.partner, embed.video, person.photo, logo.sponsor, mark.third-party), 13 `generation-safe` (placeholder, generated, user-supplied). Reference-only and blocked roles name generation-safe substitutes and carry `generatedModeGuidance`. Section fields offer `referenceRoles` and `generatedRoles` (every field has at least one generation-safe substitute, checked by `check_contracts`). Policy and availability of all 30 roles are pinned in `verify_all.py` (`PINNED`, `PINNED_AVAILABILITY`), independent of the registry. `observedAssets` maps all 464 files of the source's `public/` tree to exactly one role (check 7c).
- **Validator** (`schema/semantic_validate.py` `check_mode`): `REFERENCE_MODE_ROLE`, `GENERATED_MODE_REFERENCE_ASSET`, `GENERATED_MODE_ROLE`, `GENERATED_MODE_ASSET_REF` (`placeholder:` / `user:` / `generated:` refs only), `GENERATED_MODE_SOURCE_BRAND` (patterns), `GENERATED_MODE_SOURCE_COPY` (680 verbatim phrases of 3 or more words derived from the real source), `GENERATED_MODE_SOURCE_ENTITY` (148 people, 10 companies), `GENERATED_MODE_SOURCE_URL` (66 hosts), `GENERATED_MODE_EXTERNAL_URL` (only relative, anchor or reserved-domain URLs), `GENERATED_MODE_SOURCE_ASSET` (464 file names and the asset folders), `GENERATED_MODE_PROTECTED_VALUE` (the third-party plan mark). The lists are produced by `extraction/derive_source_protected.mjs` and traced back to the source by check 15 (every phrase and host must still occur in it).
- **Examples**: 27 PageSpecs: one reference example per template (placeholder text, `example.com`) and 13 `generated.*` examples (fictional brand, original copy, reserved `.example` URLs, generation-safe roles only), one per template. A template without a generated example, or an example whose file prefix and `mode` disagree, fails check 9.
- **Tests**: adversarial suite 217 mutations + 55 controls + 1 warn case + 21 contract and gate cases. Mode cases: logo / brand-mark roles in generated pages (8), proprietary asset roles (13), source brand text in every field type incl. route, head and refs (12), verbatim copy (8), real people and companies (5), URLs (13), asset files and refs (9), the protected plan mark (1), the `mode` field itself (4) and flips (2); plus the structure of the ten new families, retrieval pins, role wiring (6) and the wrapper gate; controls include every example and the allowed look-alikes of each leak (reserved hosts, `user:` refs, a phrase two-thirds alike). `prove_drift.py` (35 -> 110 injected defects, all caught or warned as designed, plus an untouched copy that passes) adds: availability pins, brand patterns, protection lists, observed-asset map, example leaks, the mode contract itself (validator stops enforcing generated mode / phrases / hosts / roles / refs, `mode` dropped from the schema), typography, layout, budgets, routes, IA, versions, wrapper gate. `--scratch <dir>` chooses where the scratch copies go.

### Not done / limits (stated plainly)
- **Visual parity with the live site is not measured**: there is no live-site capture pipeline here and no parity number is claimed. Check 18 only compares recorded document heights; typography, layout and motion are checked against the clone, not against the live site.
- The clone's legal, blog, changelog and app-about bodies are placeholder prose sized to the original word counts, so their budgets and heights are measured on placeholder data.
- The in-page "App not found" state and the search / pagination states are not templates (recorded in `unclassified` and `templates.json` scope).
- Generated mode checks the PageSpec, not what a renderer draws for a role; the English word "glaze" is blocked with the brand; companies named with ordinary words (Linear, Cursor, Resend) are protected only inside the longer phrases.
- The two clone defects above are pinned, not fixed (the clone's `src/` is read-only for this work).
- Measurements use the production build served locally with external requests blocked; hotlinked store images are not loaded.
